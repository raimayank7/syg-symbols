How to type symbols on a phone

Phone keyboards look small, but they hold many more symbols than they show. Almost all of them are reached in one of two ways: the symbol pages, and holding a key.

The symbol pages

Android (Gboard, the Google keyboard). Tap ?123 for numbers and common signs. On that page, tap =\< for a second page with currency signs, maths signs, brackets, ©, ®, ™, the degree sign and more. Samsung's keyboard is similar: tap !#1, then 1/2 for the second page.

iPhone and iPad. Tap 123 for numbers and common signs, then #+= for the second page with brackets, currency signs, the bullet and others.

Hold a key: the hidden rows

Hold your finger on a key for a moment and a small bar of related characters pops up. Slide to the one you want and let go. Useful ones:

  • Hold a vowel or c, n, s for accented letters: é, ü, ñ, ç.
  • Hold the currency key ($ or ₹) for the other currency signs: ₹, €, £, ¥, ¢.
  • Hold the hyphen for the en dash, the em dash and, on iPhone, the bullet.
  • Hold the full stop on the numbers page for the ellipsis (…).
  • Hold 0 (zero) on an iPhone for the degree sign (°).
  • Hold ? and ! for the inverted Spanish marks ¿ and ¡.
  • Hold the quotation mark for curly quotes and angle quotes.
  • On Gboard, hold a digit for its superscript and for fractions: holding 1 offers ¹, ½ and other fractions.
  • Hold % for the per mille sign (‰), and = for ≠ and ≈.

The exact choices differ a little between keyboard apps and versions, so try your own keys; it takes a minute and you will remember what you find.

The rupee sign

If the phone's language or region is set to India, ₹ sits on the first symbols page in place of $. Otherwise hold the $ key and slide to ₹.

Text shortcuts for symbols you use often

iPhone: Settings › General › Keyboard › Text Replacement. Tap +, paste the symbol in "Phrase" and type a short code in "Shortcut", for example tick for ✓. Android (Gboard): Settings › Dictionary › Personal dictionary, choose the language, tap +, paste the symbol as the word and type a shortcut. When you type the shortcut, the symbol is offered in the suggestion strip.

Copy and paste

For anything the keyboard does not have, such as arrows, stars, check marks, Greek letters or box-drawing lines, copy it. On this site, one tap on a symbol copies it. Then hold your finger in the text box of your app and choose Paste. Gboard and Samsung keyboards also keep a clipboard of recent copies, where you can pin symbols you need again and again.

Emoji or text?

Some symbols, such as ❤, ☀, ✔ and ⚠, exist both as plain text and as coloured emoji. Phones usually show the emoji form. You cannot always choose, because the app decides. If a symbol must stay plain and take the colour of your text, pick a neighbour that has no emoji form: ♥ instead of ❤, ✓ instead of ✔.
