Bracket and Quotation Bracket Symbols to copy and paste

44 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

Every kind of bracket: round, square and curly from the keyboard, angle brackets for mathematics, French and German quotation marks, the corner brackets used for quotations in Japanese, the heavy lenticular brackets (【 】) popular in titles, and the ceiling and floor brackets of maths.

How to use them well

Angle brackets for maths (⟨ ⟩) are not the less-than and greater-than signs (< >); the real ones are taller and thinner. The ceiling (⌈x⌉) and floor (⌊x⌋) brackets mean "round up" and "round down". The East Asian brackets are "full-width": each takes the space of a Chinese character, so they bring some empty space with them and look best around short titles, as in 【NEW】. The ornamental brackets at the end of the set are decorations and have no fixed meaning.

Windows Alt codes in this set

4 of these symbols can be typed on a Windows PC by holding Alt and typing a number on the number pad: « is Alt + 174, » is Alt + 175, ‹ is Alt + 0139, › is Alt + 0155. How it works, and the full list, is on the Alt codes page.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
