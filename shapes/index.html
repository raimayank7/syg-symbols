Geometric Shape Symbols to copy and paste

51 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

Squares, rectangles, triangles, diamonds, circles, half-filled circles and polygons, in solid and outline forms. They serve as bullets, markers on a chart key, simple progress bars and dividers.

How to use them well

A few uses: ● ○ ○ shows step one of three; ▰▰▰▱▱ makes a progress bar that works in a text message; ▲ and ▼ mark a rise and a fall in a table of prices; ◆ is a strong bullet for a heading. The half-filled circles (◐ ◑ ◒ ◓) are used for moon phases and for "partly done". The lozenge (◊) is the one shape here with a long history on calculators, where it marks a subtotal. The large squares (⬛ ⬜) are emoji and will not take the colour of your text.

Names of the first symbols

Every symbol has an official name in the Unicode standard. Knowing the name helps when you search for it on a phone or in a program's symbol picker.

  • □ white square
  • ▪ black small square
  • ▫ white small square
  • ▬ black rectangle
  • ▭ white rectangle
  • ▮ black vertical rectangle
  • ▯ white vertical rectangle
  • ▰ black parallelogram
  • ▱ white parallelogram
  • ▲ black up-pointing triangle
  • △ white up-pointing triangle
  • ▴ black up-pointing small triangle
  • ▵ white up-pointing small triangle

Windows Alt codes in this set

5 of these symbols can be typed on a Windows PC by holding Alt and typing a number on the number pad: ■ is Alt + 254, ▬ is Alt + 22, ▲ is Alt + 30, ▼ is Alt + 31, ○ is Alt + 9. How it works, and the full list, is on the Alt codes page.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
