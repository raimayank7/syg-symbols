Om, Khanda and Other Religious and Cultural Symbols to copy and paste

16 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

Symbols of faiths and ideas: Om (ॐ) in Devanagari, Ik Onkar (ੴ) in Gurmukhi, the Khanda (☬), the wheel of dharma (☸), the star and crescent (☪), the Latin and Orthodox crosses (✝ ☦), the Star of David (✡), yin and yang (☯), the peace sign (☮) and the ankh (☥), with the diya and folded hands emoji.

How to use them well

ॐ and ੴ are letters of their scripts, so they need a font for Devanagari or Gurmukhi; every modern phone has one, and they take the colour of your text. The others are drawn as coloured tiles on many phones. Treat these signs with the respect their communities give them: they belong in greetings, invitations and notices, and not on things that will be thrown on the floor or used as decoration for a joke. We have left out symbols whose meaning differs sharply between cultures.
