Roman Numeral Symbols to copy and paste

32 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

Roman numerals from one to twelve as single characters, in capital and small forms, with the signs for fifty (Ⅼ), one hundred (Ⅽ), five hundred (Ⅾ) and one thousand (Ⅿ). They are handy for clock faces, list numbering and headings in plain text.

How to use them well

These single characters exist mainly so that old East Asian texts could be stored correctly. For ordinary writing, plain capital letters (I, V, X, L, C, D, M) are the recommended way to write Roman numerals, and they are what people will type when they search. Use the single characters when you need a numeral to stay together as one unit, for example Ⅷ in a narrow table cell or a clock design. Remember the rule for reading them: a smaller value before a larger one is subtracted, so Ⅳ is four and Ⅸ is nine.
