Degree, Unit, Keyboard and Technical Symbols to copy and paste

41 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

The degree sign and temperature signs, micro and ohm, squared and cubed units as single characters, the diameter sign, the symbols printed on Mac keys (⌘ ⌥ ⇧ ⌃), return and delete keys, the power sign, and warning and recycling signs.

How to use them well

The degree sign (°) is the one most people come for. Write a temperature as 25 °C, with the sign joined to the letter; the single characters ℃ and ℉ exist but the two-character form is preferred. Do not use the masculine ordinal (º) or a raised letter o in its place: they look similar and are wrong. The diameter sign (⌀) is not the empty-set sign (∅) or the Scandinavian letter Ø. On Windows the degree sign is Alt + 0176; on a Mac it is Option + Shift + 8; on a phone, hold the zero key.

Windows Alt codes in this set

5 of these symbols can be typed on a Windows PC by holding Alt and typing a number on the number pad: ° is Alt + 248, µ is Alt + 230, Ω is Alt + 234, Å is Alt + 143, ‰ is Alt + 0137. How it works, and the full list, is on the Alt codes page.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
