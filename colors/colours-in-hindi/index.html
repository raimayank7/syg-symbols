How colour words work in Hindi

Most Hindi colour words end in आ and change with the thing they describe, like other Hindi adjectives. For a masculine noun the word ends in आ, for a feminine noun in ई, and for the plural in ए: काला घोड़ा (a black horse), काली बिल्ली (a black cat), काले जूते (black shoes). The same goes for नीला, पीला, हरा, भूरा and सुनहरा.

Some colour words never change: लाल, सफ़ेद, गुलाबी, नारंगी, बैंगनी, आसमानी and स्लेटी. So it is लाल फूल and लाल साड़ी alike.

Where the names come from

Many colour names are taken from things of that colour. नारंगी is from the orange fruit, बैंगनी from बैंगन (brinjal), गुलाबी from गुलाब (rose), आसमानी from आसमान (sky), सुनहरा from सोना (gold) and केसरिया from केसर (saffron). The English word khaki is itself from Hindustani ख़ाकी, "dust-coloured".

Useful words

  • रंग (rang): colour
  • हल्का (halka): light, as in हल्का नीला, light blue
  • गहरा (gehra): dark or deep, as in गहरा हरा, dark green
  • चमकीला (chamkeela): bright, shiny
  • फीका (pheeka): pale, faded
  • रंगीन (rangeen): colourful

Sentences to practise

  • आम पीला है। (The mango is yellow.)
  • पत्ते हरे हैं। (The leaves are green.)
  • आसमान नीला है। (The sky is blue.)
  • मेरी कमीज़ सफ़ेद है। (My shirt is white.)
  • तुम्हारा पसंदीदा रंग कौन-सा है? (Which is your favourite colour?)

Notes

Words differ from region to region. Grey is also called धूसर or सलेटी, purple जामुनी, and maroon is very often simply मैरून. In Punjabi, white is ਚਿੱਟਾ and purple ਜਾਮਨੀ. Saffron has no fixed colour code, so its sample here is only a rough guide. If a word reads oddly in your region, please tell us.
