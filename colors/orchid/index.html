About orchid

Orchid is a light, bright pinkish purple, named after the flower. It is livelier than lavender and softer than magenta.

How to mix orchid in paint

Mix purple with pink, or add a little red and blue to white.

How orchid is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For orchid it uses red 218, green 112 and blue 214 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 85%, 44% and 84% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the green light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 302°, between magenta (300°) and rose (330°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 59%, which makes it fairly strong, and its lightness is 65%, so it is light.

Lighter and darker orchid

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on orchid

White text on orchid has a contrast ratio of 2.88 : 1 and black text has 7.26 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textOrchid on white

As text on a white page, orchid has a contrast of 2.88 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to orchid are violet (#ee82ee), mediumorchid (#ba55d3), plum (#dda0dd), hotpink (#ff69b4) and palevioletred (#db7093).

Using it in a web page

color: orchid;
background: #da70d6;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 49%, 2%, 15%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of orchid?

The web colour orchid has the hex code #da70d6, which is rgb(218, 112, 214).

What colours make orchid?

Mix purple with pink, or add a little red and blue to white.

What is the opposite of orchid?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of orchid is #71da75, close to the web colour lightgreen.

Should text on orchid be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on orchid is 7.26 : 1, against 2.88 : 1 for white.
