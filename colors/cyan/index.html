About cyan

Cyan is the greenish blue made on a screen by showing green and blue light together at full strength. Web colours also call it aqua. It is one of the three ink colours of colour printing, the C of CMYK, and it is the opposite of red.

How to mix cyan in paint

In paint, mix blue with a little green and some white. Ordinary school paints can only come close, because true cyan is a printing primary that is cleaner than any mixture.

How cyan is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For cyan it uses red 0, green 255 and blue 255 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 0%, 100% and 100% of full strength. The green and blue lights are equally strong and the red light is weaker, which puts the colour midway between them.

In HSL terms its hue is 180°, exactly at cyan on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 50%, so it is of middle lightness.

Text on cyan

White text on cyan has a contrast ratio of 1.25 : 1 and black text has 16.74 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textCyan on white

As text on a white page, cyan has a contrast of 1.25 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to cyan are deepskyblue (#00bfff), darkturquoise (#00ced1), turquoise (#40e0d0), mediumturquoise (#48d1cc) and mediumspringgreen (#00fa9a).

Using it in a web page

color: cyan;
background: #00ffff;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. The name aqua is accepted for the same colour. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(100%, 0%, 0%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of cyan?

The web colour cyan has the hex code #00ffff, which is rgb(0, 255, 255).

What colours make cyan?

In paint, mix blue with a little green and some white. Ordinary school paints can only come close, because true cyan is a printing primary that is cleaner than any mixture.

What is the opposite of cyan?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of cyan is #ff0000, close to the web colour red.

Should text on cyan be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on cyan is 16.74 : 1, against 1.25 : 1 for white.
