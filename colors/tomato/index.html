About tomato

Tomato is a bright red that leans towards orange, the colour of a ripe tomato. It is a little softer than pure red and is often used for buttons and highlights.

How to mix tomato in paint

Add a little orange or yellow to red.

How tomato is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For tomato it uses red 255, green 99 and blue 71 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 100%, 39% and 28% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the blue light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 9°, between red (0°) and orange (30°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 64%, so it is of middle lightness.

Text on tomato

White text on tomato has a contrast ratio of 2.94 : 1 and black text has 7.12 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textTomato on white

As text on a white page, tomato has a contrast of 2.94 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to tomato are coral (#ff7f50), salmon (#fa8072), indianred (#cd5c5c), peru (#cd853f) and chocolate (#d2691e).

Using it in a web page

color: tomato;
background: #ff6347;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 61%, 72%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of tomato?

The web colour tomato has the hex code #ff6347, which is rgb(255, 99, 71).

What colours make tomato?

Add a little orange or yellow to red.

What is the opposite of tomato?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of tomato is #47e3ff, close to the web colour turquoise.

Should text on tomato be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on tomato is 7.12 : 1, against 2.94 : 1 for white.
