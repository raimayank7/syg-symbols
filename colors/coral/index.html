About coral

Coral is a pinkish orange, named after sea coral. It is warm and lively and is popular for clothes, lipsticks and summer designs. It pairs well with teal and navy.

How to mix coral in paint

Mix red and yellow to make orange, then add white and a little more red or pink.

How coral is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For coral it uses red 255, green 127 and blue 80 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 100%, 50% and 31% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the blue light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 16°, between red (0°) and orange (30°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 66%, so it is light.

Lighter and darker coral

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on coral

White text on coral has a contrast ratio of 2.49 : 1 and black text has 8.4 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textCoral on white

As text on a white page, coral has a contrast of 2.49 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to coral are tomato (#ff6347), salmon (#fa8072), sandybrown (#f4a460), lightcoral (#f08080) and darksalmon (#e9967a).

Using it in a web page

color: coral;
background: #ff7f50;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 50%, 69%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of coral?

The web colour coral has the hex code #ff7f50, which is rgb(255, 127, 80).

What colours make coral?

Mix red and yellow to make orange, then add white and a little more red or pink.

What is the opposite of coral?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of coral is #52d1ff, close to the web colour mediumturquoise.

Should text on coral be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on coral is 8.4 : 1, against 2.49 : 1 for white.
