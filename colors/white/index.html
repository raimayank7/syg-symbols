About white

White is all colours of light together. On a screen it is red, green and blue at full strength. In paint it is the lightest pigment and is used to make tints of every other colour. White reflects heat, so white clothes and whitewashed roofs are cooler in summer.

How to mix white in paint

White cannot be mixed from other paints. It is the paint you add to others. Slightly coloured whites are made by adding a trace of another colour: yellow for ivory and cream, blue for a cool white.

How white is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For white it uses red 255, green 255 and blue 255 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 100%, 100% and 100% of full strength. All three lights are equal, which is what makes a colour with no hue: black, white or a grey.

In HSL terms its hue is 0°, which has no meaning for a colour without hue. Its saturation is 0%, which makes it almost without colour, close to a grey, and its lightness is 100%, so it is very light.

Lighter and darker white

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Colours that go with white

White has no hue, so the colour wheel has nothing to say about it. That is its strength: it goes with every colour. Pair it with one strong colour and it will make that colour stand out. Some good partners are shown below.

Text on white

White text on white has a contrast ratio of 1 : 1 and black text has 21 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

As text on a white page, white has a contrast of 1 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to white are snow (#fffafa), ghostwhite (#f8f8ff), mintcream (#f5fffa), azure (#f0ffff) and ivory (#fffff0).

Using it in a web page

color: white;
background: #ffffff;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 0%, 0%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of white?

The web colour white has the hex code #ffffff, which is rgb(255, 255, 255).

What colours make white?

White cannot be mixed from other paints. It is the paint you add to others. Slightly coloured whites are made by adding a trace of another colour: yellow for ivory and cream, blue for a cool white.

What is the opposite of white?

As a colour without hue, white has no opposite on the colour wheel. In lightness, the opposite of black is white.

Should text on white be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on white is 21 : 1, against 1 : 1 for white.
