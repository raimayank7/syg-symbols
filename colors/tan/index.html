About tan

Tan is a pale brown, the colour of tanned leather. It is a common colour for shoes, belts, bags and upholstery, and a warm neutral for backgrounds.

How to mix tan in paint

Add white to brown, with a little yellow to keep it warm.

How tan is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For tan it uses red 210, green 180 and blue 140 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 82%, 71% and 55% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the blue light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 34°, between orange (30°) and yellow (60°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 44%, which makes it fairly strong, and its lightness is 69%, so it is light.

Lighter and darker tan

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on tan

White text on tan has a contrast ratio of 1.97 : 1 and black text has 10.64 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textTan on white

As text on a white page, tan has a contrast of 1.97 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to tan are burlywood (#deb887), darkkhaki (#bdb76b), darksalmon (#e9967a), rosybrown (#bc8f8f) and darkgray (#a9a9a9).

Using it in a web page

color: tan;
background: #d2b48c;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 14%, 33%, 18%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of tan?

The web colour tan has the hex code #d2b48c, which is rgb(210, 180, 140).

What colours make tan?

Add white to brown, with a little yellow to keep it warm.

What is the opposite of tan?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of tan is #8dabd3, close to the web colour skyblue.

Should text on tan be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on tan is 10.64 : 1, against 1.97 : 1 for white.
