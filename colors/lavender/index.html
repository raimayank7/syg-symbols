About lavender

Lavender, as a web colour, is a very pale bluish purple, close to white. It is named after the flower, whose real colour is a stronger purple. It is used as a soft background and in designs meant to feel calm.

How to mix lavender in paint

Add a little purple to plenty of white. A trace of blue keeps it cool.

How lavender is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For lavender it uses red 230, green 230 and blue 250 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 90%, 90% and 98% of full strength. The blue light is the strongest and the green light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 240°, exactly at blue on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 67%, which makes it fairly strong, and its lightness is 94%, so it is very light.

Lighter and darker lavender

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on lavender

White text on lavender has a contrast ratio of 1.23 : 1 and black text has 17.06 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textLavender on white

As text on a white page, lavender has a contrast of 1.23 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to lavender are aliceblue (#f0f8ff), whitesmoke (#f5f5f5), ghostwhite (#f8f8ff), lightcyan (#e0ffff) and azure (#f0ffff).

Using it in a web page

color: lavender;
background: #e6e6fa;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(8%, 8%, 0%, 2%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of lavender?

The web colour lavender has the hex code #e6e6fa, which is rgb(230, 230, 250).

What colours make lavender?

Add a little purple to plenty of white. A trace of blue keeps it cool.

What is the opposite of lavender?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of lavender is #fafae5, close to the web colour oldlace.

Should text on lavender be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on lavender is 17.06 : 1, against 1.23 : 1 for white.
