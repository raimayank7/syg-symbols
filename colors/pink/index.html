About pink

Pink is a pale red. It is named after a flower, the pink, a relative of the carnation. Light pinks feel soft and gentle, and strong pinks such as hot pink are loud and lively. Jaipur is called the Pink City for the colour of its old buildings.

How to mix pink in paint

Add a little red to white. Always put the red into the white, a drop at a time, because a small amount of red goes a long way. A touch of yellow makes a warmer, salmon pink.

How pink is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For pink it uses red 255, green 192 and blue 203 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 100%, 75% and 80% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the green light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 350°, between rose (330°) and red (360°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 88%, so it is very light.

Lighter and darker pink

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on pink

White text on pink has a contrast ratio of 1.53 : 1 and black text has 13.65 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textPink on white

As text on a white page, pink has a contrast of 1.53 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to pink are lightpink (#ffb6c1), peachpuff (#ffdab9), bisque (#ffe4c4), wheat (#f5deb3) and thistle (#d8bfd8).

Using it in a web page

color: pink;
background: #ffc0cb;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 25%, 20%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of pink?

The web colour pink has the hex code #ffc0cb, which is rgb(255, 192, 203).

What colours make pink?

Add a little red to white. Always put the red into the white, a drop at a time, because a small amount of red goes a long way. A touch of yellow makes a warmer, salmon pink.

What is the opposite of pink?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of pink is #c2fff5, close to the web colour paleturquoise.

Should text on pink be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on pink is 13.65 : 1, against 1.53 : 1 for white.
