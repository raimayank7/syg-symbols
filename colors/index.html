What this site is for

What do I mix to get it? Will people be able to read text on it? This site answers those four questions.

Each well-known colour has its own page with its codes, tints and shades, matching colours, how to mix it in paint and which text colour to use on it. The tools work for any colour at all.

A note on screens

A colour code tells a screen how much red, green and blue light to show. Screens differ in brightness and tint, so the same code can look a little different on a phone, a laptop and a projector, and different again when printed. For anything that must match exactly, such as paint or a printed logo, compare real samples in daylight.

Common questions

How do I convert HEX to RGB?

Split the hex code into three pairs and change each pair from base 16: multiply the first digit by 16 and add the second. For #ffa500 the pairs ff, a5 and 00 give 255, 165 and 0. Or type the code in the converter.

What two colours make green?

In paint, blue and yellow. On a screen green is itself a primary colour of light. Red and yellow make orange, and red and blue make purple.

What is the opposite of a colour?

The colour directly across the colour wheel, called its complement. On the screen wheel the opposite of red is cyan and of blue is yellow. On the painter's wheel the opposite of red is green.

Does the converter store my colours?

No. It works inside your browser. Nothing you type or pick is sent anywhere.
