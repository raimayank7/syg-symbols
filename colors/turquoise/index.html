About turquoise

Turquoise is a light greenish blue, named after the gemstone, which reached Europe through Turkey. It is the colour of shallow tropical seas and is common in jewellery and tiles.

How to mix turquoise in paint

Mix blue with a little green and plenty of white. More green moves it towards aquamarine.

How turquoise is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For turquoise it uses red 64, green 224 and blue 208 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 25%, 88% and 82% of full strength. The green light is the strongest and the red light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 174°, between spring green (150°) and cyan (180°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 72%, which makes it fairly strong, and its lightness is 56%, so it is of middle lightness.

Text on turquoise

White text on turquoise has a contrast ratio of 1.64 : 1 and black text has 12.79 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textTurquoise on white

As text on a white page, turquoise has a contrast of 1.64 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Using it in a web page

color: turquoise;
background: #40e0d0;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(71%, 0%, 7%, 12%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of turquoise?

The web colour turquoise has the hex code #40e0d0, which is rgb(64, 224, 208).

What colours make turquoise?

Mix blue with a little green and plenty of white. More green moves it towards aquamarine.

What is the opposite of turquoise?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of turquoise is #e03e4e, close to the web colour indianred.

Should text on turquoise be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on turquoise is 12.79 : 1, against 1.64 : 1 for white.
