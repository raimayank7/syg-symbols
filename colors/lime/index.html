About lime

Lime, in web colours, is pure green light at full strength. It is the brightest green a screen can show. Away from screens, lime green means a sharp yellowish green like the fruit. It is hard to read as text on white.

How to mix lime in paint

Mix yellow with a little green or blue, keeping the yellow in charge. Add white for a pale lime.

How lime is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For lime it uses red 0, green 255 and blue 0 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 0%, 100% and 0% of full strength. The green light is the strongest and the blue light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 120°, exactly at green on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 50%, so it is of middle lightness.

Lighter and darker lime

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on lime

White text on lime has a contrast ratio of 1.37 : 1 and black text has 15.3 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textLime on white

As text on a white page, lime has a contrast of 1.37 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to lime are limegreen (#32cd32), lawngreen (#7cfc00), forestgreen (#228b22), chartreuse (#7fff00) and green (#008000).

Using it in a web page

color: lime;
background: #00ff00;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(100%, 0%, 100%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of lime?

The web colour lime has the hex code #00ff00, which is rgb(0, 255, 0).

What colours make lime?

Mix yellow with a little green or blue, keeping the yellow in charge. Add white for a pale lime.

What is the opposite of lime?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of lime is #ff00ff, close to the web colour fuchsia.

Should text on lime be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on lime is 15.3 : 1, against 1.37 : 1 for white.
