About sky blue

Sky blue is the light blue of a clear daytime sky. The Hindi word for it is aasmani. It is restful and widely used for shirts, walls and backgrounds.

How to mix sky blue in paint

Add a little blue to plenty of white.

How sky blue is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For sky blue it uses red 135, green 206 and blue 235 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 53%, 81% and 92% of full strength. The blue light is the strongest and the red light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 197°, between cyan (180°) and azure (210°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 71%, which makes it fairly strong, and its lightness is 73%, so it is light.

Text on sky blue

White text on sky blue has a contrast ratio of 1.74 : 1 and black text has 12.05 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textSky blue on white

As text on a white page, sky blue has a contrast of 1.74 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to sky blue are lightskyblue (#87cefa), lightblue (#add8e6), lightsteelblue (#b0c4de), powderblue (#b0e0e6) and paleturquoise (#afeeee).

Using it in a web page

color: skyblue;
background: #87ceeb;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(43%, 12%, 0%, 8%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of sky blue?

The web colour skyblue has the hex code #87ceeb, which is rgb(135, 206, 235).

What colours make sky blue?

Add a little blue to plenty of white.

What is the opposite of sky blue?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of sky blue is #eba589, close to the web colour darksalmon.

Should text on sky blue be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on sky blue is 12.05 : 1, against 1.74 : 1 for white.
