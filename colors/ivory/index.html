About ivory

Ivory is an off-white with a faint yellow warmth, named after the material of elephant tusks. It is softer than pure white and is often chosen for wedding cards, paper and walls.

How to mix ivory in paint

Add the smallest touch of yellow to white.

How ivory is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For ivory it uses red 255, green 255 and blue 240 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 100%, 100% and 94% of full strength. The red and green lights are equally strong and the blue light is weaker, which puts the colour midway between them.

In HSL terms its hue is 60°, exactly at yellow on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 97%, so it is very light.

Text on ivory

White text on ivory has a contrast ratio of 1 : 1 and black text has 20.81 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textIvory on white

As text on a white page, ivory has a contrast of 1 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to ivory are floralwhite (#fffaf0), seashell (#fff5ee), snow (#fffafa), mintcream (#f5fffa) and oldlace (#fdf5e6).

Using it in a web page

color: ivory;
background: #fffff0;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 0%, 6%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of ivory?

The web colour ivory has the hex code #fffff0, which is rgb(255, 255, 240).

What colours make ivory?

Add the smallest touch of yellow to white.

What is the opposite of ivory?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of ivory is #f0f0ff, close to the web colour aliceblue.

Should text on ivory be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on ivory is 20.81 : 1, against 1 : 1 for white.
