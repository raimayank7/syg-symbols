About black

Black is the absence of light. On a screen it is all three lights switched off. In paint and ink it is the darkest pigment. It gives the strongest contrast with white, which is why most text is printed in black. It is a formal colour for clothes and a common one for cars, phones and shoes.

How to mix black in paint

A true black is best bought. A very dark near-black can be mixed from blue and brown, or from all three primaries in strong amounts, and such a mixed black often looks richer in a painting than black from the tube.

How black is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For black it uses red 0, green 0 and blue 0 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 0%, 0% and 0% of full strength. All three lights are equal, which is what makes a colour with no hue: black, white or a grey.

In HSL terms its hue is 0°, which has no meaning for a colour without hue. Its saturation is 0%, which makes it almost without colour, close to a grey, and its lightness is 0%, so it is very dark.

Colours that go with black

Black has no hue, so the colour wheel has nothing to say about it. That is its strength: it goes with every colour. Pair it with one strong colour and it will make that colour stand out. Some good partners are shown below.

Text on black

White text on black has a contrast ratio of 21 : 1 and black text has 1 : 1, so white text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to black are darkgreen (#006400), midnightblue (#191970), darkslategray (#2f4f4f), darkslategrey (#2f4f4f) and green (#008000).

Using it in a web page

color: black;
background: #000000;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 0%, 0%, 100%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of black?

The web colour black has the hex code #000000, which is rgb(0, 0, 0).

What colours make black?

A true black is best bought. A very dark near-black can be mixed from blue and brown, or from all three primaries in strong amounts, and such a mixed black often looks richer in a painting than black from the tube.

What is the opposite of black?

As a colour without hue, black has no opposite on the colour wheel. In lightness, the opposite of black is white.

Should text on black be black or white?

White text. Its contrast on black is 21 : 1, against 1 : 1 for black.
