About beige

Beige is a very pale sandy colour, somewhere between cream and light brown. The word is French and first meant undyed wool. It is one of the most used wall and fabric colours because it is warm without drawing attention.

How to mix beige in paint

Add a very little brown to white, with a trace of yellow.

How beige is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For beige it uses red 245, green 245 and blue 220 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 96%, 96% and 86% of full strength. The red and green lights are equally strong and the blue light is weaker, which puts the colour midway between them.

In HSL terms its hue is 60°, exactly at yellow on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 56%, which makes it fairly strong, and its lightness is 91%, so it is very light.

Lighter and darker beige

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on beige

White text on beige has a contrast ratio of 1.1 : 1 and black text has 18.97 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textBeige on white

As text on a white page, beige has a contrast of 1.1 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to beige are cornsilk (#fff8dc), antiquewhite (#faebd7), lightgoldenrodyellow (#fafad2), linen (#faf0e6) and oldlace (#fdf5e6).

Using it in a web page

color: beige;
background: #f5f5dc;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 0%, 10%, 4%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of beige?

The web colour beige has the hex code #f5f5dc, which is rgb(245, 245, 220).

What colours make beige?

Add a very little brown to white, with a trace of yellow.

What is the opposite of beige?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of beige is #dbdbf5, close to the web colour lavender.

Should text on beige be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on beige is 18.97 : 1, against 1.1 : 1 for white.
