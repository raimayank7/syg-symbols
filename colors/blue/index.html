About blue

Blue is a primary colour of both light and paint. It is the colour of a clear sky and of deep water, and surveys in many countries find it the most commonly named favourite colour. Dark blues are widely used for uniforms, and blue ink is the usual choice for writing.

How to mix blue in paint

Blue is a primary colour in paint and cannot be mixed. Add white for sky blue, black for navy, yellow for green and red for purple.

How blue is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For blue it uses red 0, green 0 and blue 255 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 0%, 0% and 100% of full strength. The blue light is the strongest and the green light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 240°, exactly at blue on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 50%, so it is of middle lightness.

Lighter and darker blue

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on blue

White text on blue has a contrast ratio of 8.59 : 1 and black text has 2.44 : 1, so white text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to blue are mediumblue (#0000cd), darkblue (#00008b), navy blue (#000080), royalblue (#4169e1) and indigo (#4b0082).

Using it in a web page

color: blue;
background: #0000ff;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(100%, 100%, 0%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of blue?

The web colour blue has the hex code #0000ff, which is rgb(0, 0, 255).

What colours make blue?

Blue is a primary colour in paint and cannot be mixed. Add white for sky blue, black for navy, yellow for green and red for purple.

What is the opposite of blue?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of blue is #ffff00, close to the web colour yellow.

Should text on blue be black or white?

White text. Its contrast on blue is 8.59 : 1, against 2.44 : 1 for black.
