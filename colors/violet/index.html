About violet

Violet is the last colour of the rainbow, at the short-wave end of visible light. It is named after the flower. The web colour called violet is a light pinkish purple, lighter than what most people would call violet.

How to mix violet in paint

Mix blue and red with more blue than red. Add white to reach the lighter violet of web colours.

How violet is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For violet it uses red 238, green 130 and blue 238 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 93%, 51% and 93% of full strength. The red and blue lights are equally strong and the green light is weaker, which puts the colour midway between them.

In HSL terms its hue is 300°, exactly at magenta on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 76%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 72%, so it is light.

Lighter and darker violet

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on violet

White text on violet has a contrast ratio of 2.31 : 1 and black text has 9.06 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textViolet on white

As text on a white page, violet has a contrast of 2.31 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to violet are orchid (#da70d6), plum (#dda0dd), hotpink (#ff69b4), thistle (#d8bfd8) and lightpink (#ffb6c1).

Using it in a web page

color: violet;
background: #ee82ee;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 45%, 0%, 7%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of violet?

The web colour violet has the hex code #ee82ee, which is rgb(238, 130, 238).

What colours make violet?

Mix blue and red with more blue than red. Add white to reach the lighter violet of web colours.

What is the opposite of violet?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of violet is #81ee81, close to the web colour lightgreen.

Should text on violet be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on violet is 9.06 : 1, against 2.31 : 1 for white.
