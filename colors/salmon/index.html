About salmon

Salmon is a soft orange-pink, named after the flesh of the fish. It is gentler than coral and often used for walls, cards and clothes.

How to mix salmon in paint

Mix pink with a little orange or yellow, or add a touch of red and yellow to white.

How salmon is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For salmon it uses red 250, green 128 and blue 114 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 98%, 50% and 45% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the blue light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 6°, between red (0°) and orange (30°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 93%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 71%, so it is light.

Lighter and darker salmon

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on salmon

White text on salmon has a contrast ratio of 2.5 : 1 and black text has 8.39 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textSalmon on white

As text on a white page, salmon has a contrast of 2.5 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to salmon are lightcoral (#f08080), darksalmon (#e9967a), lightsalmon (#ffa07a), coral (#ff7f50) and sandybrown (#f4a460).

Using it in a web page

color: salmon;
background: #fa8072;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 49%, 54%, 2%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of salmon?

The web colour salmon has the hex code #fa8072, which is rgb(250, 128, 114).

What colours make salmon?

Mix pink with a little orange or yellow, or add a touch of red and yellow to white.

What is the opposite of salmon?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of salmon is #70ecfa, close to the web colour lightskyblue.

Should text on salmon be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on salmon is 8.39 : 1, against 2.5 : 1 for white.
