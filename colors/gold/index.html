About gold

Gold, as a web colour, is a deep rich yellow. It cannot show the shine of the metal, which comes from the way metal reflects light, so on screens the shine is suggested with gradients. Gold is used for prizes, borders and festive designs.

How to mix gold in paint

Mix yellow with a little orange or light brown. For the look of metal you need metallic paint, since shine cannot be mixed.

How gold is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For gold it uses red 255, green 215 and blue 0 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 100%, 84% and 0% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the blue light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 51°, between orange (30°) and yellow (60°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 50%, so it is of middle lightness.

Lighter and darker gold

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on gold

White text on gold has a contrast ratio of 1.4 : 1 and black text has 14.97 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textGold on white

As text on a white page, gold has a contrast of 1.4 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to gold are yellow (#ffff00), orange (#ffa500), goldenrod (#daa520), darkorange (#ff8c00) and greenyellow (#adff2f).

Using it in a web page

color: gold;
background: #ffd700;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 16%, 100%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of gold?

The web colour gold has the hex code #ffd700, which is rgb(255, 215, 0).

What colours make gold?

Mix yellow with a little orange or light brown. For the look of metal you need metallic paint, since shine cannot be mixed.

What is the opposite of gold?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of gold is #0026ff, close to the web colour blue.

Should text on gold be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on gold is 14.97 : 1, against 1.4 : 1 for white.
