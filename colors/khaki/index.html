About khaki

Khaki is a light dusty yellow-brown. The word is Hindustani and means "dust-coloured". It was first used for army uniforms in India in the nineteenth century and spread across the world. The web colour khaki is paler and yellower than uniform khaki.

How to mix khaki in paint

Mix yellow with a little brown and a good amount of white. A trace of green gives the army version.

How khaki is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For khaki it uses red 240, green 230 and blue 140 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 94%, 90% and 55% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the blue light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 54°, between orange (30°) and yellow (60°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 77%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 75%, so it is light.

Text on khaki

White text on khaki has a contrast ratio of 1.28 : 1 and black text has 16.4 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textKhaki on white

As text on a white page, khaki has a contrast of 1.28 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Using it in a web page

background: #f0e68c;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 4%, 42%, 6%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of khaki?

The web colour khaki has the hex code #f0e68c, which is rgb(240, 230, 140).

What colours make khaki?

Mix yellow with a little brown and a good amount of white. A trace of green gives the army version.

What is the opposite of khaki?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of khaki is #8e98f0, close to the web colour cornflowerblue.

Should text on khaki be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on khaki is 16.4 : 1, against 1.28 : 1 for white.
