About brown

Brown is a dark orange: the colour of soil, wood, tea and chocolate. It is not on the simple colour wheel, because it is made by darkening or dulling a warm colour. Browns are steady background colours that sit well with almost anything.

How to mix brown in paint

Mix all three primaries, red, yellow and blue, with rather more red and yellow than blue. Another way is to mix orange with a little blue, or red with green. Add white for tan and beige.

How brown is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For brown it uses red 165, green 42 and blue 42 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 65%, 16% and 16% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the blue light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 0°, exactly at red on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 59%, which makes it fairly strong, and its lightness is 41%, so it is of middle lightness.

Lighter and darker brown

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on brown

White text on brown has a contrast ratio of 7.08 : 1 and black text has 2.96 : 1, so white text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

As text on a white page, brown has a contrast of 7.08 : 1. It reads well. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to brown are firebrick (#b22222), sienna (#a0522d), saddlebrown (#8b4513), crimson (#dc143c) and darkred (#8b0000).

Using it in a web page

color: brown;
background: #a52a2a;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 75%, 75%, 35%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of brown?

The web colour brown has the hex code #a52a2a, which is rgb(165, 42, 42).

What colours make brown?

Mix all three primaries, red, yellow and blue, with rather more red and yellow than blue. Another way is to mix orange with a little blue, or red with green. Add white for tan and beige.

What is the opposite of brown?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of brown is #2ba6a6, close to the web colour lightseagreen.

Should text on brown be black or white?

White text. Its contrast on brown is 7.08 : 1, against 2.96 : 1 for black.
