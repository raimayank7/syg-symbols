About teal

Teal is a dark blue-green, named after a duck that has a stripe of this colour on its head. It sits exactly between blue and green and pairs well with white, grey, coral and gold.

How to mix teal in paint

Mix blue and green in about equal parts and darken with a little black. White turns it into a soft sea green.

How teal is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For teal it uses red 0, green 128 and blue 128 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 0%, 50% and 50% of full strength. The green and blue lights are equally strong and the red light is weaker, which puts the colour midway between them.

In HSL terms its hue is 180°, exactly at cyan on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 25%, so it is dark.

Text on teal

White text on teal has a contrast ratio of 4.77 : 1 and black text has 4.39 : 1, so white text is the one to use. That is enough for normal text at level AA of the accessibility guidelines.

Using it in a web page

color: teal;
background: #008080;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(100%, 0%, 0%, 50%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of teal?

The web colour teal has the hex code #008080, which is rgb(0, 128, 128).

What colours make teal?

Mix blue and green in about equal parts and darken with a little black. White turns it into a soft sea green.

What is the opposite of teal?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of teal is #800000, close to the web colour maroon.

Should text on teal be black or white?

White text. Its contrast on teal is 4.77 : 1, against 4.39 : 1 for black.
