About navy blue

Navy is a very dark blue, named after the colour of naval uniforms. It is the usual dark blue of school uniforms, blazers and trousers, and it is often chosen in place of black because it is a little softer.

How to mix navy blue in paint

Add a little black to blue. A trace of red or purple gives a deeper midnight blue.

How navy blue is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For navy blue it uses red 0, green 0 and blue 128 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 0%, 0% and 50% of full strength. The blue light is the strongest and the green light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 240°, exactly at blue on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 25%, so it is dark.

Text on navy blue

White text on navy blue has a contrast ratio of 16 : 1 and black text has 1.31 : 1, so white text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

As text on a white page, navy blue has a contrast of 16 : 1. It reads well. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to navy blue are darkblue (#00008b), midnightblue (#191970), indigo (#4b0082), mediumblue (#0000cd) and darkslateblue (#483d8b).

Using it in a web page

color: navy;
background: #000080;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(100%, 100%, 0%, 50%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of navy blue?

The web colour navy has the hex code #000080, which is rgb(0, 0, 128).

What colours make navy blue?

Add a little black to blue. A trace of red or purple gives a deeper midnight blue.

What is the opposite of navy blue?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of navy blue is #808000, close to the web colour olive.

Should text on navy blue be black or white?

White text. Its contrast on navy blue is 16 : 1, against 1.31 : 1 for black.
