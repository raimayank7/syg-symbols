About chocolate

Chocolate, as a web colour, is a medium orange-brown, lighter than a bar of dark chocolate. It is a warm colour for wood, leather and food designs.

How to mix chocolate in paint

Mix red, yellow and a little blue to make brown, with the red and yellow in the lead.

How chocolate is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For chocolate it uses red 210, green 105 and blue 30 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 82%, 41% and 12% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the blue light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 25°, between red (0°) and orange (30°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 75%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 47%, so it is of middle lightness.

Lighter and darker chocolate

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Text on chocolate

White text on chocolate has a contrast ratio of 3.63 : 1 and black text has 5.77 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is enough for normal text at level AA of the accessibility guidelines.

White textBlack textChocolate on white

As text on a white page, chocolate has a contrast of 3.63 : 1. It is fine for large headings but too faint for small text. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to chocolate are darkgoldenrod (#b8860b), peru (#cd853f), sienna (#a0522d), goldenrod (#daa520) and tomato (#ff6347).

Using it in a web page

color: chocolate;
background: #d2691e;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 50%, 86%, 18%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of chocolate?

The web colour chocolate has the hex code #d2691e, which is rgb(210, 105, 30).

What colours make chocolate?

Mix red, yellow and a little blue to make brown, with the red and yellow in the lead.

What is the opposite of chocolate?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of chocolate is #1e87d2, close to the web colour dodgerblue.

Should text on chocolate be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on chocolate is 5.77 : 1, against 3.63 : 1 for white.
