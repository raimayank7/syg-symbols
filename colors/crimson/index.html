About crimson

Crimson is a strong, deep red that leans slightly towards blue. The name goes back to a dye made from an insect. It is used where red needs to look rich and serious, as on blazers, flags and book covers.

How to mix crimson in paint

Add a small touch of blue or purple to red.

How crimson is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For crimson it uses red 220, green 20 and blue 60 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 86%, 8% and 24% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the green light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 348°, between rose (330°) and red (360°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 83%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 47%, so it is of middle lightness.

Text on crimson

White text on crimson has a contrast ratio of 4.98 : 1 and black text has 4.2 : 1, so white text is the one to use. That is enough for normal text at level AA of the accessibility guidelines.

As text on a white page, crimson has a contrast of 4.98 : 1. It reads well. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to crimson are firebrick (#b22222), brown (#a52a2a), red (#ff0000), mediumvioletred (#c71585) and indianred (#cd5c5c).

Using it in a web page

color: crimson;
background: #dc143c;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 91%, 73%, 14%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of crimson?

The web colour crimson has the hex code #dc143c, which is rgb(220, 20, 60).

What colours make crimson?

Add a small touch of blue or purple to red.

What is the opposite of crimson?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of crimson is #14dbb4, close to the web colour darkturquoise.

Should text on crimson be black or white?

White text. Its contrast on crimson is 4.98 : 1, against 4.2 : 1 for black.
