About grey

Grey lies between black and white and has no hue of its own. The spelling is grey in India and Britain and gray in America, and web colours accept both. Grey is the standard neutral in design: text, borders and backgrounds are very often shades of grey.

How to mix grey in paint

Mix black and white. For a livelier grey, mix two opposite colours such as blue and orange with white. A trace of blue gives a cool grey, and a trace of brown a warm one.

How grey is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For grey it uses red 128, green 128 and blue 128 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 50%, 50% and 50% of full strength. All three lights are equal, which is what makes a colour with no hue: black, white or a grey.

In HSL terms its hue is 0°, which has no meaning for a colour without hue. Its saturation is 0%, which makes it almost without colour, close to a grey, and its lightness is 50%, so it is of middle lightness.

Lighter and darker grey

Tints are made by adding white, shades by adding black. Tap a box to copy its code.

Colours that go with grey

Grey has no hue, so the colour wheel has nothing to say about it. That is its strength: it goes with every colour. Pair it with one strong colour and it will make that colour stand out. Some good partners are shown below.

Text on grey

White text on grey has a contrast ratio of 3.94 : 1 and black text has 5.31 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is enough for normal text at level AA of the accessibility guidelines.

As text on a white page, grey has a contrast of 3.94 : 1. It is fine for large headings but too faint for small text. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to grey are slategray (#708090), slategrey (#708090), lightslategray (#778899), lightslategrey (#778899) and dimgray (#696969).

Using it in a web page

color: gray;
background: #808080;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. The name grey is accepted for the same colour. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 0%, 0%, 50%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of grey?

The web colour gray has the hex code #808080, which is rgb(128, 128, 128).

What colours make grey?

Mix black and white. For a livelier grey, mix two opposite colours such as blue and orange with white. A trace of blue gives a cool grey, and a trace of brown a warm one.

What is the opposite of grey?

As a colour without hue, grey has no opposite on the colour wheel. In lightness, the opposite of black is white.

Should text on grey be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on grey is 5.31 : 1, against 3.94 : 1 for white.
