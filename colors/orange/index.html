About orange

Orange is a secondary colour that sits between red and yellow. It takes its name from the fruit. It is easy to see against sky and water, which is why life jackets and traffic cones are orange. A deeper shade of it, saffron, has a special place in India.

How to mix orange in paint

Mix red and yellow in equal parts. More yellow gives a golden orange, and more red gives a deep reddish orange. A touch of white softens it to peach.

How orange is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For orange it uses red 255, green 165 and blue 0 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 100%, 65% and 0% of full strength. The red light is the strongest and the blue light the weakest.

In HSL terms its hue is 39°, between orange (30°) and yellow (60°) on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 50%, so it is of middle lightness.

Text on orange

White text on orange has a contrast ratio of 1.97 : 1 and black text has 10.63 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

White textBlack textOrange on white

As text on a white page, orange has a contrast of 1.97 : 1. It is too faint to read: use it for shapes and backgrounds, not for words. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to orange are darkorange (#ff8c00), goldenrod (#daa520), gold (#ffd700), darkgoldenrod (#b8860b) and chocolate (#d2691e).

Using it in a web page

color: orange;
background: #ffa500;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 35%, 100%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of orange?

The web colour orange has the hex code #ffa500, which is rgb(255, 165, 0).

What colours make orange?

Mix red and yellow in equal parts. More yellow gives a golden orange, and more red gives a deep reddish orange. A touch of white softens it to peach.

What is the opposite of orange?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of orange is #0059ff, close to the web colour dodgerblue.

Should text on orange be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on orange is 10.63 : 1, against 1.97 : 1 for white.
