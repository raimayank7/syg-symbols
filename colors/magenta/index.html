About magenta

Magenta is the pinkish purple made on a screen by showing red and blue light together at full strength. Web colours also call it fuchsia. It is the M of CMYK printing and the opposite of green. Unusually, it matches no single colour of the rainbow: the eye sees it when red and blue arrive together.

How to mix magenta in paint

In paint, mix red with a little purple or blue and some white. As with cyan, school paints can only come close to the clean magenta of printing ink.

How magenta is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For magenta it uses red 255, green 0 and blue 255 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 100%, 0% and 100% of full strength. The red and blue lights are equally strong and the green light is weaker, which puts the colour midway between them.

In HSL terms its hue is 300°, exactly at magenta on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 50%, so it is of middle lightness.

Text on magenta

White text on magenta has a contrast ratio of 3.13 : 1 and black text has 6.69 : 1, so black text is the one to use. That is enough for normal text at level AA of the accessibility guidelines.

White textBlack textMagenta on white

As text on a white page, magenta has a contrast of 3.13 : 1. It is fine for large headings but too faint for small text. Check any pair in the contrast checker.

Similar named colours

The closest of the 148 named web colours to magenta are deeppink (#ff1493), darkviolet (#9400d3), mediumorchid (#ba55d3), darkorchid (#9932cc) and orchid (#da70d6).

Using it in a web page

color: magenta;
background: #ff00ff;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. The name fuchsia is accepted for the same colour. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 100%, 0%, 0%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of magenta?

The web colour magenta has the hex code #ff00ff, which is rgb(255, 0, 255).

What colours make magenta?

In paint, mix red with a little purple or blue and some white. As with cyan, school paints can only come close to the clean magenta of printing ink.

What is the opposite of magenta?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of magenta is #00ff00, close to the web colour lime.

Should text on magenta be black or white?

Black text. Its contrast on magenta is 6.69 : 1, against 3.13 : 1 for white.
