About purple

Purple lies between red and blue. For centuries purple dye was rare and costly, so the colour came to stand for royalty and wealth. In everyday speech purple and violet are used for the same colours, though strictly violet is bluer.

How to mix purple in paint

Mix red and blue. Use a cool red, one that leans to pink and not to orange, or the result turns brownish. More blue gives violet, more red gives a wine purple, and white gives lilac and lavender.

How purple is made on a screen

A screen mixes red, green and blue light. For purple it uses red 128, green 0 and blue 128 on a scale of 0 to 255, which is 50%, 0% and 50% of full strength. The red and blue lights are equally strong and the green light is weaker, which puts the colour midway between them.

In HSL terms its hue is 300°, exactly at magenta on the colour wheel. Its saturation is 100%, which makes it vivid, and its lightness is 25%, so it is dark.

Text on purple

White text on purple has a contrast ratio of 9.41 : 1 and black text has 2.22 : 1, so white text is the one to use. That is strong enough for text of any size.

Using it in a web page

color: purple;
background: #800080;

The name and the hex code mean exactly the same colour to a browser. For print, the simple CMYK conversion is cmyk(0%, 100%, 0%, 50%), but a print shop's own figures will differ: see RGB and CMYK.

Common questions

What is the hex code of purple?

The web colour purple has the hex code #800080, which is rgb(128, 0, 128).

What colours make purple?

Mix red and blue. Use a cool red, one that leans to pink and not to orange, or the result turns brownish. More blue gives violet, more red gives a wine purple, and white gives lilac and lavender.

What is the opposite of purple?

On the screen colour wheel the opposite of purple is #008000, close to the web colour green.

Should text on purple be black or white?

White text. Its contrast on purple is 9.41 : 1, against 2.22 : 1 for black.
