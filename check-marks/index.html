Check Mark, Tick and Cross Symbols to copy and paste

17 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

Ticks for "yes", "done" and "correct", crosses for "no" and "wrong", and empty boxes for lists that will be ticked by hand. The light tick (✓) and heavy tick (✔) are plain text. The green box tick (✅) and red cross (❌) are emoji and always appear in colour.

How to use them well

For a to-do list in a document, pair the empty ballot box (☐) with the ticked box (☑) or the crossed box (☒). In India a tick means "correct" and a cross means "wrong", but that is not true everywhere: on forms in some countries, including many in the United States, an X in a box is how you select an option. If your list will be read abroad, add the words "yes" and "no". Word has no simple key for the tick; copying it from here is the quickest way.

Names of the first symbols

Every symbol has an official name in the Unicode standard. Knowing the name helps when you search for it on a phone or in a program's symbol picker.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
