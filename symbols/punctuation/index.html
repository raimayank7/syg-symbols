Punctuation and Typography Symbols to copy and paste

43 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

The marks that printers use and keyboards leave out: the true ellipsis, the en dash and em dash, curly quotation marks in English, German and French styles, the inverted Spanish marks, the section and paragraph signs, daggers for footnotes, primes for feet and inches, and the Hindi full stop (।) called the danda.

How to use them well

Three pairs are mixed up most often. A hyphen (-) joins words; an en dash (–) shows a range, as in 2019–2024; an em dash is a break in a sentence. Straight quotes (' ") are typewriter marks; curly quotes (‘ ’ “ ”) are the printed form, and word processors usually convert them for you. The prime (′) is for feet and minutes and the double prime (″) for inches and seconds; they are not apostrophes. In Hindi, end a sentence with the danda, not a full stop.

Windows Alt codes in this set

23 of these symbols can be typed on a Windows PC by holding Alt and typing a number on the number pad: … is Alt + 0133, – is Alt + 0150, — is Alt + 0151, ‘ is Alt + 0145, ’ is Alt + 0146, ‚ is Alt + 0130, “ is Alt + 0147, ” is Alt + 0148, „ is Alt + 0132, « is Alt + 174. How it works, and the full list, is on the Alt codes page.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
