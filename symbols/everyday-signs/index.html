Everyday Signs: Male, Female, Recycle, Warning, Phone and More to copy and paste

29 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

Signs from maps, labels and notices: male and female, the recycling arrows, the warning triangle, anchor, aeroplane, telephone, envelope, scissors, pencil, house, flags, hot drink, wheelchair, football, sailing boat, fuel pump, alarm clock and a few tools.

How to use them well

These are handy in contact lines (☎ 98xxx, ✉ name@example.com), in timetables (✈ for flights), on forms ("✂ cut here") and in notices. The male (♂) and female (♀) signs come from the old symbols for Mars and Venus. The recycling sign (♻) in text only shows the idea of recycling; the numbered triangles on plastic are separate marks set by industry standards. Many of these become coloured emoji on phones. A warning sign in a document should always be followed by the warning in words.

Windows Alt codes in this set

3 of these symbols can be typed on a Windows PC by holding Alt and typing a number on the number pad: ♂ is Alt + 11, ♀ is Alt + 12, ⌂ is Alt + 127. How it works, and the full list, is on the Alt codes page.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
