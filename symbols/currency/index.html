More about it

What is in this set

Money signs from around the world: the Indian rupee, dollar, euro, pound, yen and yuan, the won, rouble, lira, dong, peso, naira, shekel, hryvnia, tenge, taka, baht and more, plus the bitcoin sign. The older generic rupee sign (₨) is still used for the Pakistani, Sri Lankan, Nepalese and Mauritian rupees.

How to use them well

The Indian rupee sign (₹) was adopted by the Government of India in 2010. It goes before the amount with no space in most Indian usage: ₹500. If the sign shows as an empty box on an old device, write "Rs" or "INR" instead. In invoices and anything that crosses borders, the three-letter code (INR, USD, EUR) is safer than a sign, because $ alone can mean the dollar of a dozen countries and ¥ can mean yen or yuan.

Windows Alt codes in this set

6 of these symbols can be typed on a Windows PC by holding Alt and typing a number on the number pad: € is Alt + 0128, £ is Alt + 156, ¥ is Alt + 157, ¢ is Alt + 155, ƒ is Alt + 159, ¤ is Alt + 0164. How it works, and the full list, is on the Alt codes page.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
