Heart Symbols to copy and paste

25 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

Text hearts and emoji hearts in one place. The first row is made of plain characters: the black heart suit (♥), the white heart (♡), the heavy heart (❤) and a few leafy "floral hearts" used by printers as ornaments. They take the colour of the text around them. The rest are emoji in fixed colours.

How to use them well

The plain ♥ comes from playing cards and is the safest choice: it shows on every device made in the last twenty years. The heavy heart ❤ turns red on most phones, because phones treat it as an emoji. Coloured hearts have picked up loose meanings in chat, such as yellow for friendship and blue for trust, but these are fashions, not rules, and they differ from group to group. On Windows, Alt + 3 on the number pad types ♥.

Windows Alt codes in this set

1 of these symbols can be typed on a Windows PC by holding Alt and typing a number on the number pad: ♥ is Alt + 3. How it works, and the full list, is on the Alt codes page.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
