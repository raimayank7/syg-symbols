Music Note Symbols to copy and paste

14 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

Notes and signs from written music: the quarter note (♩), eighth note (♪), beamed notes (♫ ♬), flat (♭), natural (♮) and sharp (♯), the treble and bass clefs, and the note emoji. People use them to mark song lyrics, playlists and music-class notes.

How to use them well

The sharp sign (♯) is not the hash sign (#). In ♯ the upright strokes are straight and the cross strokes slant, so that they are not lost against the lines of the staff; in # it is the other way round. Typing C# is fine in a message, but in printed music theory use C♯ and B♭. The clef characters come from a part of Unicode that older devices do not support, so they may show as a box. On Windows, Alt + 13 types ♪ and Alt + 14 types ♫.

Windows Alt codes in this set

2 of these symbols can be typed on a Windows PC by holding Alt and typing a number on the number pad: ♪ is Alt + 13, ♫ is Alt + 14. How it works, and the full list, is on the Alt codes page.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
