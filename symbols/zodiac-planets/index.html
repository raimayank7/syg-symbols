Zodiac and Planet Symbols to copy and paste

23 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

The twelve signs of the western zodiac in order, from Aries (♈) to Pisces (♓), followed by the old symbols for the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto.

How to use them well

The planet symbols are far older than the telescope and are still used in astronomy tables. Two of them have a second life: the Venus sign (♀) stands for female and the Mars sign (♂) for male in biology. Most phones draw the twelve zodiac signs as purple emoji tiles. If you need the plain glyph, for a printed chart for example, set the text in a symbol font on a computer. The Indian rashi names follow the same order: Mesh, Vrishabh, Mithun, Kark, Simha, Kanya, Tula, Vrishchik, Dhanu, Makar, Kumbh, Meen.

Windows Alt codes in this set

2 of these symbols can be typed on a Windows PC by holding Alt and typing a number on the number pad: ♀ is Alt + 12, ♂ is Alt + 11. How it works, and the full list, is on the Alt codes page.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
