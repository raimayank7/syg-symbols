Star and Flower Symbols to copy and paste

46 symbols. Tap one to copy it; its name and codes appear at the bottom of the screen.

More about it

What is in this set

Stars, sparkles, asterisks, snowflakes and small flowers. The black star (★) and white star (☆) are the pair used for ratings: ★★★★☆ is four out of five. The four-pointed sparkles (✦ ✧) are popular in profile names and headings, and the florets (✿ ❀) decorate greetings.

How to use them well

Most of these come from an old printer's font called Dingbats, and they are drawn in the colour of your text, so they suit both light and dark screens. The glowing star (⭐) is different: it is an emoji and always appears yellow. Use decorations lightly in anything a screen reader may read aloud, because each one is spoken by its full name. A row of ten stars is ten spoken words.

Every symbol has an official name in the Unicode standard. Knowing the name helps when you search for it on a phone or in a program's symbol picker.

If a symbol shows as a box

Your device has no drawing for it in its fonts. Newer phones show nearly all of these; the reasons and fixes are in why symbols show as boxes.
